01 · The place

The village, measured.

Duino is a village of a few streets between the Karst and the sea. From the gate you walk to the little harbour, the Rilke Path and the Castle, where in January 1912 Rilke began the Duino Elegies: these homes take their name from there. By car, Trieste and the airport.

How it is calculated

Calculated on the outer glazing of each veranda (for home 1, on the edge of the porch), one and a half metres up, every five minutes, with the terrain (the Region's LiDAR), the neighbouring houses and the building itself. The second figure also counts the trees as solid crowns: the strictest count, since in winter many crowns are bare. No clouds; sunrise and sunset are those on a clear horizon.

04 · How it is built

Built in layers.

As you scroll, the building goes up as it will on site, from the foundations to the roof.

1Foundations

Upturned reinforced-concrete beams.

Sized on the geological report and the structural calculations; under the ground-floor slab, a ventilated air gap.

2Structure

X-LAM timber walls and floors.

Cross-laminated spruce panels, CE-marked. The roof rests on glulam beams; in some first-floor rooms the timber is left exposed.

3Envelope

Rock wool outside, laminated glass.

The insulation does not burn (class A1). Thermally broken windows in anthracite grey, with laminated low-emissivity double glazing.

4Roof

Solar panels for every home.

The flat roof, ballasted with river pebbles, carries each home's solar panels. On the street side, vertical-slat shutters.

5Outside

Gardens, hedges, parking.

Evergreen hedges and native Karst plants; parking in permeable concrete, each space wired for an electric-car charger.

And inside, no gas.

  • An air-to-water heat pump: heating, cooling and hot water
  • Solar panels for every home, up to 6 kW, with its own meter
  • Energy class A NZEB by design
  • Armoured front door (anti-burglary class 3 or higher), video intercom, pre-wiring for alarm and home automation
