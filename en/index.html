01 · The place

Between castle, cliffs and the open sea.

A short walk from the Castle of Duino — historic seat of the Princes of Thurn und Taxis — and from the Sentiero Rilke, the cliff-top path overlooking the Gulf, Elegie Duino occupies a rare position where the Carso landscape meets the Adriatic.

Minutes on foot from Duino's marina, restaurants and lidos; ten minutes from the beaches of Sistiana and Portopiccolo; fifteen from Trieste airport at Ronchi dei Legionari. Trieste, Slovenia and the A4 motorway are within easy reach.

The surroundings include the Duino Cliffs Nature Reserve, the Miramare Marine Protected Area — a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve — and the Vitovska and Terrano vineyards of the Carso.

02 · The project

Eight residences, one vision.

Eight residential units arranged over two above-ground levels: one- and two-bedroom layouts, each with dedicated parking. Ground-floor units feature a private garden; first-floor units a terrace.

The architecture favours pure volumes, flat roofs and generous glazing, fostering continuity between indoors and outdoors. The light, textured façades converse with the Carso landscape; vertical wood-effect composite louvres introduce an elegant rhythm and contribute to solar control.

03 · Architecture & materials

Built to last. Designed to live.

The load-bearing structure is built entirely in PEFC-certified X-LAM (Cross Laminated Timber) spruce panels: solid timber with cross-laminated layers — a natural, renewable material delivering high seismic and thermo-acoustic performance.

Thick rockwool external thermal insulation, thermal-break aluminium windows (RAL 7016) with low-emissivity double glazing, vertical composite louvres. Materials compliant with the Italian Minimum Environmental Criteria.

  • A.

    X-LAM structure in PEFC spruce

    Cross-laminated solid timber load-bearing panels. CE marking and ETA certification. Dry-construction system, shorter site times, high seismic performance.

  • B.

    Rockwool thermal envelope

    Thick external insulation, fire-reaction class A1, excellent thermo-acoustic performance in both summer and winter.

  • C.

    Heat pump + photovoltaic

    Each residence equipped with an air-to-water heat pump, recessed or surface fan coils, dedicated rooftop photovoltaic system and EV-charging provision at the parking space.

04 · Interiors

Continuity between indoors and out.

In the principal rooms — living and bedrooms — full-height glazing opens onto the private gardens or terraces, expanding the space and welcoming natural light.

A sober, material-led aesthetic: premium oak parquet in the sleeping areas, stone- or concrete-effect porcelain stoneware in the living spaces and bathrooms, dark-grey aluminium frames.

07 · Technical specification

Technical highlights, in detail.

Fourteen sections of signed specification — from foundations to landscape, from plant systems to finishing materials. The full brochure is available for download; the integral technical specification is provided on request during negotiation.

01

Structure · PEFC X-LAM spruce

Cross-laminated solid timber load-bearing panels for walls and slabs. Glued-laminated timber roof with double planking. CE marking, ETA certification, PEFC-traced supply chain.

02

Envelope · Rockwool ETICS

Thick rockwool external thermal insulation, acrylic-siloxane render in warm dark grey as per landscape authorisation. Fire-reaction class A1.

03

Windows · Aluminium RAL 7016

Thermal-break aluminium windows and French doors, laminated low-emissivity insulating glass with PVB safety interlayer. Composite louvres on the street façade, fabric blinds on south and west elevations.

04

Flooring · Oak parquet and stoneware

Pre-finished oak parquet 15×190 cm in bedrooms; porcelain stoneware 30×60 / 60×60 cm in living areas and bathrooms, stone or concrete effect. Coloured-in-mass grout sealing.

05

HVAC · Air-to-water heat pump

Autonomous system per unit: heating, cooling and DHW via high-efficiency heat pump with 200 L storage. Design fan-coil units, remote control via app.

06

Electrical · Photovoltaic + EV charging

Dedicated photovoltaic system per unit, EV-charging provision at each parking space, video-intercom with call recording, fibre, data and Wi-Fi provision.
