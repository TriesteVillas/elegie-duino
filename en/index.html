02 · The name

January 1912.Duino Castle

Rainer Maria Rilke has been the guest of Princess Marie von Thurn und Taxis at Duino Castle since October 1911.

One morning, the Princess recalls, a violent bora is blowing but the sun is out, and the sea is "as if spun over with silver". Rilke is pacing the bastions below the Castle, high above the rocks, thinking about how to answer a tiresome letter. He stops: in the roar of the wind a voice seems to call out a line to him. By that evening he has the first elegy.

The elegies would become ten. He finished them in February 1922, in Switzerland, and published them in 1923 under the name of the place where they had begun.

Around

The cliffs, the reserve, the sea.

Below the village lies the Duino Cliffs Nature Reserve: 107 hectares since 1996, the only high sea cliff on Italy's northern Adriatic coast. The Rilke Path follows it for about 1.7 km, to the bay of Sistiana. Further east, towards Trieste, the Miramare Marine Protected Area.

How it is calculated

Calculated on the outer glazing of each veranda (for home 1, on the edge of the porch), one and a half metres up, every five minutes, with the terrain (the Region's LiDAR), the neighbouring houses and the building itself. The second figure also counts the trees as solid crowns: the strictest count, since in winter many crowns are bare. No clouds; sunrise and sunset are those on a clear horizon.

08 · How it is built

Built in layers.

As you scroll, the building goes up as it will on site, from the foundations to the roof. Each layer in the words of the specification.

Today

The plot, after the demolition.

Before building started, the empty plot was scanned in 3D: you can walk where the homes will stand.

1Foundations

Upturned reinforced-concrete beams.

Under the building, reinforced-concrete foundations with upturned beams, sized on the geological report and the structural calculations. Between the ground and the ground-floor slab, a ventilated air gap.

travi rovesce, dimensionate in conformità alle risultanze della relazione geologica e del calcolo strutturale — from the specification
2X-LAM walls

Cross-laminated spruce.

Everything above ground is carried by solid timber panels: kiln-dried spruce boards glued with crossed grain, CE-marked and with a European Technical Assessment. The same panel forms the outer wall and the wall between homes, with layers that separate them acoustically.

il legno impiegato proviene esclusivamente da foreste gestite in modo sostenibile e certificate PEFC — from the specification
3Floors

Timber overhead too.

Between the two floors the slab is X-LAM again; the roof rests on glulam beams with a double cross boarding, left exposed in some first-floor rooms. Above the slabs, a lightweight screed that carries pipes and cables.

struttura in travi in legno lamellare […] abbinata a un doppio tavolato incrociato — from the specification
4Insulation

Rock wool, outside.

The walls are wrapped in an external rock-wool insulation: it does not burn (class A1), meets Italy's Minimum Environmental Criteria, and its thickness comes from the energy calculations. The real finish is a dark warm grey render; here the model stays white, like a maquette.

tonalità grigio caldo scuro (tipo Sandtex n. 36) come da progetto approvato in sede di autorizzazione paesaggistica — from the specification
5Windows

Laminated glass, anthracite frames.

Thermally broken windows and French windows in anthracite grey RAL 7016, with laminated low-emissivity double glazing: if a pane breaks, it stays in one piece. The first-floor verandas have metal railings in the same colour.

vetrocamera stratificata in lastre di vetro 3+3 mm interno, camera 14/16 mm, vetro 4+4 mm esterno — from the specification
6Shutters

Vertical slats on the street side.

On the north side, towards the street, the shutters run beside the tall windows: vertical slats in composite material, wood-effect in the architect's elevations. The same slats at the shared entrance.

realizzati in composito a doghe verticali — from the specification
7Roof

A roof that works.

The flat roof is waterproofed with two crossed membranes and ballasted with river pebbles. It holds each home's solar panels and the screened outdoor units of the heat pumps. Edges and gutters in anthracite aluminium.

Sulle porzioni di tetto piano saranno alloggiati gli impianti fotovoltaici dedicati a ciascuna unità immobiliare — from the specification
8Outside

Gardens, hedges, parking.

Each ground-floor home has its own garden, enclosed by evergreen hedges, planted with native Karst species that need little water. Parking in permeable concrete, each space wired for an EV charger; motorised driveway gates.

Ciascuna unità al piano terra sarà dotata di giardino privato di pertinenza esclusiva — from the specification
