01 · The project

Eight residences between castle, cliffs and the open sea.

A short walk from the Castle of Duino and the Sentiero Rilke cliff-top path, Elegie Duino occupies a rare position where the Carso landscape meets the Adriatic. Eight units over two levels — one- and two-bedroom layouts, each with dedicated parking — with a private garden on the ground floor and a terrace on the first.

Pure volumes, flat roofs and generous glazing foster continuity between indoors and outdoors: oak parquet in the sleeping areas, stone-effect stoneware in the living spaces, full-height glazing onto gardens and terraces. NZEB Class A.

Minutes on foot from Duino's marina, restaurants and lidos; ten minutes from the beaches of Sistiana and Portopiccolo; fifteen from Trieste airport at Ronchi dei Legionari. Trieste, Slovenia and the A4 motorway are within easy reach.

The surroundings include the Duino Cliffs Nature Reserve, the Miramare Marine Protected Area — a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve — and the Vitovska and Terrano vineyards of the Carso.

03 · Technical specification

Technical highlights, in detail.

Fourteen sections of signed specification — from foundations to landscape, from plant systems to finishing materials. The full brochure is available for download; the integral technical specification is provided on request during negotiation.

01

Structure · PEFC X-LAM spruce

Cross-laminated solid timber load-bearing panels for walls and slabs. Glued-laminated timber roof with double planking. CE marking, ETA certification, PEFC-traced supply chain.

02

Envelope · Rockwool ETICS

Thick rockwool external thermal insulation, acrylic-siloxane render in warm dark grey as per landscape authorisation. Fire-reaction class A1.

03

Windows · Aluminium RAL 7016

Thermal-break aluminium windows and French doors, laminated low-emissivity insulating glass with PVB safety interlayer. Composite louvres on the street façade, fabric blinds on south and west elevations.

04

Flooring · Oak parquet and stoneware

Pre-finished oak parquet 15×190 cm in bedrooms; porcelain stoneware 30×60 / 60×60 cm in living areas and bathrooms, stone or concrete effect. Coloured-in-mass grout sealing.

05

HVAC · Air-to-water heat pump

Autonomous system per unit: heating, cooling and DHW via high-efficiency heat pump with 200 L storage. Design fan-coil units, remote control via app.

06

Electrical · Photovoltaic + EV charging

Dedicated photovoltaic system per unit, EV-charging provision at each parking space, video-intercom with call recording, fibre, data and Wi-Fi provision.
